OBJECTIVES

1. To develop a Python-based academic performance tracking application.

2. To collect student and subject information.

3. To calculate subject-wise academic performance.

4. To automatically assign grades based on marks.

5. To record and check student attendance.

6. To generate a simple academic performance report.

7. To use basic Python concepts such as functions, lists, dictionaries, loops and conditional statements.

8. To extend the application in future modules with GPA, CGPA and graphical visualization.
