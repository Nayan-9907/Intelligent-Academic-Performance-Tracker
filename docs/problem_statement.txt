PROBLEM STATEMENT
Students need to regularly keep track of their marks, grades and attendance in different subjects. Calculating this information manually can take time and may result in calculation errors. It can also be difficult to understand overall academic performance from individual subject marks.
The Intelligent Academic Performance Tracker & Visualizer is proposed as a Python-based application to simplify this process. The system collects student details, subject-wise marks and attendance, performs basic calculations and displays an academic performance summary.
The project will be developed in multiple modules. The first module focuses on basic academic tracking, while the future modules will add GPA/CGPA calculation and graphical visualization of performance.
